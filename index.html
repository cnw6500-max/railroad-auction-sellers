A marketplace only for railroad images

  • Buyers browse by railroad, format, equipment, decade and state, or search for a road number or a place.
  • Every railroad has its own page, and every seller has a page showing their open auctions and ratings.
  • Collectors can ask to be emailed when a new item from their railroad is listed.

What bidders see on your listing

  • Bidders enter the most they will pay and the site bids for them in small steps.
  • A bid in the last two minutes extends the auction, so last-second sniping does not cut the price short.
  • You can set a hidden reserve and a Buy Now price.
  • The price and time left update live, and bidders can ask you questions on the page.

List one slide or a whole box

  • Five steps for a single item: photos, railroad, item details, auction settings, review.
  • Bulk upload creates many auctions at once from a spreadsheet and a folder of scans, including a CSV exported from SixBit.
  • Edit or end a listing until the first bid, and relist anything that does not sell.

How you get paid

  1. The buyer pays the site by card within 4 days.
  2. We email you the shipping address. Do not ship before then.
  3. You ship and enter the carrier and tracking number.
  4. Your payout is released when the buyer confirms receipt, or 7 days after you mark it shipped.
  5. Stripe deposits it in your bank account.

What the site takes care of

Sales tax

Charged to the buyer at checkout and sent to the states by the site, not by you.

Winners who do not pay

A reminder, then an offer to the next bidder. Three misses blocks a bidder.

Problems with an order

A buyer's report goes to the site, which reviews it and decides on a refund.

Your reputation

Buyers leave star ratings that show on your seller page and on every listing.

Bringing bidders back

Emails for new listings that match an alert, outbid notices and ending-soon reminders.

Your records

Each sale on your dashboard shows the price, the charges and your payout.
